turn 3 · colour-coded by data type · icons throughout · mascot central

Every data type now carries its own colour and icon, so a row is legible before it is read: rose for milk, lilac for breast, amber for formula, yellow for pee, mint for poop, periwinkle for notes. Icons are Material Symbols Rounded. Liana sits beside the big number rather than replacing it; her states are descriptive only — settled · awake · hungry · sleeping, plus a one-off logged flash after a save. The four-column table still stays plain enough to read against a photo of the paper page.
